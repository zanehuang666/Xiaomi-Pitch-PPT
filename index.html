Executive Summary

Industry

Overview

Two-sided Industry Reset: Cost Inflation and Value Migration

Supply-side: AI infrastructure CapEx is tightening memory supply, with DRAM and NAND expected to reach 43% of the reference smartphone BoM by Q3 2026E, up from 13% in Q1 2025. DRAM is expected to remain constrained into 2027 as capacity prioritises HBM and server demand, while NAND supply could partially ease in 2H27.

Demand-side: Weak shipment growth and component inflation are shifting competition from volume to value, forcing low-end portfolio rationalisation and higher ASPs, while increasing the importance of pricing power.

Structural Supply-chain Reconfiguration Amid Geopolitical Fragmentation

Expansions overseas are transferring from stage #1 volumn-based competition to stage #2 sustainable building

Manufacturing diversification is evolving beyond assembly relocation towards local sourcing and regional operations with higher execution risks.

Company

Overview

Global Smartphone Franchise with Established Market Leadership

Xiaomi ranked No.3 globally with 11.5% shipment share in Q2 2026, ranking Top 3 in 53 markets and Top 5 in 67 markets. Over 640 overseas new retail stores support continued market penetration.

smartphone ASP reached RMB1,351 in Q2 2026, while the segment gross margin remained under pressure at 8.5%.

Four-layer Consumer Ecosystem with Distinct Strategic Roles

Smartphones — Acquisition: The primary gateway to Xiaomi's 766.5mn global monthly active users.

AIoT — Attachment: 1.16bn connected devices deepen ecosystem penetration and cross-selling opportunities.

Internet Services — Monetisation: 76.8% Q2 gross margin and 32% of group gross profit provide a high-margin earnings pool.

Smart EV — Extension: 104k Q2 deliveries expand consumer touchpoints and potential lifetime value, although profitability remains under development.

What We

Like

Premiumisation

Near-term defence: Higher launch prices and low-end portfolio optimisation help absorb memory inflation and protect unit economics.

Long-term opportunity: Flagship innovation and premium mix expansion can lift pricing power and widen Xiaomi's addressable market, creating potential margin upside once component costs stabilise.

Ecosystem Flywheel

Hardware expansion: Smartphones acquire users, while AIoT and EVs increase device attachment, engagement and potential customer lifetime value.

Services monetisation: Xiaomi's high-margin Internet business offers a potential long-term profit-mix opportunity, analogous to Apple's historical shift towards services; AI-enabled experiences could further strengthen retention and monetisation.

Global Scale

Strong positions in Southeast Asia, Latin America and Europe, alongside expanding overseas retail channels, provide a platform to introduce higher-value smartphones and additional AIoT categories.

Process

&

Next

Steps

Margin Recovery: Validate Pricing Power Against Memory Cost Inflation

Track DRAM/NAND prices, smartphone BoM, ASP and segment gross margins to assess pricing pass-through and the timing of margin recovery.

Ecosystem Monetisation: Verify the Conversion from User Scale to Profit

Monitor connected-device growth, user engagement, Internet services ARPU, gross margin and contribution from advertising, gaming and other services.

Growth Sustainability and Valuation: Separate Core Earnings from Long-term Optionality

Assess smartphone premiumisation, overseas market execution and IoT profitability as core earnings drivers.

Evaluate EV investment requirements, profitability and cash-flow contribution separately, while testing whether current valuation adequately reflects sustainable earnings growth.
